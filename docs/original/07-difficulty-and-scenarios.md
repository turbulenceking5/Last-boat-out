# Difficulty and scenarios

## Difficulty

| Difficulty | Orders a turn | Infection | Ships |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reservist | 6 | New outbreaks 30% smaller | 20% bigger |
| Regular | 5 | As designed | As designed |
| Veteran | 4 | Starts one infection-rate step higher (outbreaks likelier and bigger from the start); outbreaks 20% bigger | 15% smaller |

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Outbreak | Varies |
|---|---|---|
| Kessler | The eastern city, Kessler, with infected already in St Brennan's and one nearby place | The campaign as designed |
| Random outbreak | One of Kessler, Northgate, Ashford, Marrow, St Brennan's, Camp Torrin or Harlow Junction | Port capacities (±25%), two roads lost to storm damage, and the timing of several events (±1 turn) |

## Seeds

Every campaign has a seed. Enter one on the setup screen to replay a campaign, or share it with a link like `https://turbulenceking5.github.io/Last-boat-out/#seed=4242&scenario=random&diff=veteran`. Opening that link fills in the setup screen. The same seed and the same orders give the same campaign.
