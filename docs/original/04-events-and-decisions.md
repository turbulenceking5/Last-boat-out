# Events and decisions

Scripted events interrupt the campaign and ask you to choose. Turn numbers are when an event first becomes possible (the Random scenario shifts some by a turn either way); some also need conditions to be true.

| Event | From turn | Choices |
|---|---|---|
| **You have command** | 0 | The briefing |
| **The Kessler question** (the origin city) | 2 | **Seal the city:** blow its links; public order falls, the cabinet approves. **Keep the roads open:** evacuate it; columns may carry the infection. |
| **Dr Okafor's research** | 3 | **Send a battalion** (lost for good) or **fly them out by helicopter** (2 sorties): a treatment in 3 turns. **Refuse.** |
| **The last broadcast from St Brennan's** | If you refused Dr Okafor and the hospital is falling | **Send the helicopter** (1 sortie): half the time it gets through, treatment in 4 turns, confidence +3 either way. **Tell her we can't reach her:** confidence −4, the treatment is lost. |
| **A breakthrough** | When the treatment arrives | Nearly half of the bitten are saved: infection slows everywhere |
| **The mayor's request** | 5 | **Promise to hold** a threatened town for 4 turns (kept: confidence +10; broken: −12), or **make no promises** (−3) |
| **The grid is failing** | 7 | Keep one thing lit at night: **the roads to the ports** (columns don't suffer worse losses at night; order +2), **the line** (unfortified garrisons don't weaken at night), or **the field hospitals** (infection spreads 15% slower; order +4) |
| **Fuel crisis** | 8 | **Requisition the fuel:** +40 supply, evacuations halved for 4 turns, public order falls. **Ration the army:** rail moves cost 2 orders for the rest of the war. |
| **The bishop's request** | 9 | **Promise to empty** a large town within 6 turns (starts its evacuation; kept +10, broken −12), or **make no promises** (−3) |
| **The coalition wants its troops back** | 10 | **Let them go:** lose 2 battalions in 2 turns, Southport ships +6k a turn, confidence +4. **Refuse:** confidence −10. |
| **The Navy can send more ships** | 12 | Send a flotilla to one port: +12k a turn there for the rest of the evacuation |
| **The harbourmasters** | 14 | **Promise to hold a port** to the end (judged when the last ships sail; confidence +3 now), or **make no promises** (−3) |
| **Camp Torrin asks to fall back** | 15 | **Fall back to Port Calder:** Torrin's garrison joins Calder, which gains a fortification level. **Hold Torrin:** a fortification level and 2 reserve battalions. |
| **The great march** | 16 | Warning: the biggest horde (25k or more) will surge out along every open road |
| **The last ships** | 22 | Warning: whoever is on the quays at dawn on Day 13 gets out |

Promises are the surest way to raise confidence, and breaking them is the surest way to lose it.
