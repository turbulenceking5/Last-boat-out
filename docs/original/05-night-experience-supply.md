# Night, experience and supply

## Night

Every second turn is a night. At night:

- hordes spill further along the roads; by day they move more slowly
- unfortified garrisons that aren't yet veterans fight at 85%
- refugee columns caught by the infected suffer worse losses

The forecast arrows already allow for this. The **grid is failing** decision can remove one of these penalties.

## Experience

Garrisons that kill infected gain experience: **green, blooded, veteran, elite**.

- Each level adds 12% killing power and cuts the garrison's own losses.
- Veterans and better ignore the night penalty.
- Troops carry their experience when they move. Fresh troops, militia and reinforcements dilute it.
- The army bases (Fort Brannock and Camp Torrin) start blooded.
- Having an elite garrison earns a medal.

## Supply

- Places with production (Aldermoor 6, Hollis Farms 4, Larch Depot 4, each port 3, Harlow Junction 2) add supply each turn while they stand and have people.
- Every battalion connected to Aldermoor by an unbroken route costs 1 supply a turn. If there isn't enough, everyone goes on rations and fights worse.
- A garrison cut off from Aldermoor uses any supply air-dropped to it. Without that, it weakens every turn: it fights worse, and loses troops.

## The gauges

| Gauge | Effect |
|---|---|
| Public order | Below 35, evacuations move half as many people; below 15, almost none |
| Government confidence | 80 or more gives an extra order each turn; below 30 costs one; at 0 you are relieved of command |

## Infection rate

The infection speeds up on turns 6, 12 and 18. Each step makes new outbreaks from infected travellers more likely and bigger.
