The map has 41 links, 11 of them railways:

- the main line west to east: Westhaven, Aldermoor, Cedar Gap, Larch Depot, St Brennan's, Kessler, Port Calder
- south from Aldermoor through Fort Brannock to Southport
- Ashford through Harlow Junction to Kessler
- Camp Torrin to Port Calder

Troops can move several stops along unbroken railway in one order, as long as no place on the way has infected.
