# How to play Last Boat to Calder

**Play:** https://turbulenceking5.github.io/Last-boat-out/

You are the general in charge of Vessmark, a fictional country, during the first two weeks of a zombie outbreak. You command from the capital, Aldermoor. The Navy is sending ships to three ports: Westhaven, Southport and Port Calder. **The last ships sail at dawn on Day 13.**

## A turn

- Each turn is 12 hours, and there are 24 turns. Every second turn is a night.
- You get a handful of **orders** each turn: 5 on Regular, 6 on Reservist, 4 on Veteran. High government confidence gives one more; low confidence takes one away.
- Select a place on the map (or in the list) to see it and give orders there. See [Orders](02-orders.md).
- Press **End turn** (or E). Twelve hours pass: hordes spread and fight, refugees walk, ships sail, and events may ask you to decide something.

## Reading the map

- Places are coloured by state: secure, threatened, infected and holding, infected and losing, fallen, or empty.
- **Red arrows** show where each horde will move when you end the turn. A **"!"** marks where infected travellers will start a new outbreak.
- Battalions show as blue badges. Refugee columns are white dots walking toward a port.
- Roads and railways link the 21 places. Blowing one is permanent.

## Winning

You are scored on the share of Vessmark's people you saved. See [Scoring and medals](06-scoring-and-medals.md).
