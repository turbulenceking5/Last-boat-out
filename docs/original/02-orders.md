# Orders

Select a place to see the orders available there. A greyed-out order says why it can't be given (for example "Needs 8 supply, you have 5"). U undoes your last order this turn.

| Order | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Move troops** | 1 order (2 by rail if the army is on fuel rations) | Move 1, half or all battalions to a neighbour by road, or further along unbroken railway through places with no infected |
| **Evacuate civilians** | 1 order; stopping is free (refunded if ordered this turn) | People leave in refugee columns for the nearest open port, by the route shown. A port evacuates straight onto its own quay. |
| **Fortify** | 1 order and 8 supply | +1 fortification level, up to 3. Not while infected. Each level makes the garrison 50% stronger and cuts its losses. |
| **Air-drop supplies** | 1 order and 10 supply | 10 supply to a place, which keeps a cut-off garrison fighting |
| **Raise militia** | 1 order | 10k civilians become 1 battalion. Needs at least 15k people. Public order −2. |
| **Air strike** | 2 orders and 1 sortie | Kills up to 20k infected, and 15% of the civilians there |
| **Helicopter airlift** | 1 order and 1 sortie | Flies up to 6k civilians from a place that isn't a port onto the least crowded open quay. The only way out for a town cut off from every port. |
| **Blow a road or bridge** | 1 order | Cuts a link permanently. At most 2 a turn. Hordes, refugees and supply can't cross it. |

You have **5 air sorties** for the whole campaign, shared by air strikes and airlifts.

## Reinforcements

From the third turn: Aldermoor gains 1 battalion a turn, Fort Brannock 0.75, and Camp Torrin 0.5 every second turn, as long as they stand.
