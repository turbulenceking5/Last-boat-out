# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **k** | Thousands of people, in the original game: "35k" is 35,000 |
| **inf** | Infected |
| **bn** | Battalion, in the original game. Each kills about 2,000 infected a turn when supplied. |
| **Order** | One action in a turn of the original game. You get 4 to 6 a turn. |
| **Sortie** | One use of air power in the original game: 5 for the whole campaign, shared by air strikes and airlifts |
| **Horde** | A mass of infected at one place (original) or moving together (City) |
| **Spark** | A new outbreak started by infected travellers, marked "!" on the original's map |
| **The great march** | In the original, the biggest horde surging out along every open road |
| **The great horde** | In the City, 220 infected arriving from the east at minute 8 |
| **Quay** | The waterfront where evacuees wait for ships |
| **Held line** | In the original, a place whose garrison is strong enough to hold off its infected |
| **Fallen** | A place overrun by the infected (original) |
| **Overrun** | A City block whose people have all been caught. It turns dark and smokes. |
| **Breach** | In the City, the moment the infected batter their way through a block's doors |
| **Figure** | In the City, one person-shaped figure on the map, standing for 10 people |
| **Walker, runner, brute** | The City's three kinds of infected |
| **Evacuation point** | Where evacuees wait: the harbour, or a bus point in the City |
| **APC** | Armoured personnel carrier. In the City it fights, and carries up to 60 people. |
| **Mission** | A pop-up objective in the City: a supply drop or survivors |
| **Seed** | The number that fixes a campaign's random events, so it can be replayed or shared |
| **Experience** | In the original, a garrison's level: green, blooded, veteran, elite |
| **Public order, confidence** | The original's two gauges. Order affects evacuation speed; confidence affects orders per turn, and at 0 ends the game. |
