# 04. Game overview

From the README, the code and the first two screens.

- **Setting:** Vessmark, a fictional country, in the first two weeks of a zombie outbreak. You command from the capital, Aldermoor.
- **Length:** 24 turns of 12 hours each. The last ships sail at dawn on Day 13.
- **Map:** 21 places joined by roads and railways: towns, army bases, supply depots and three ports (Westhaven, Southport, Port Calder).
- **Orders each turn** (5 on Regular, 6 on Reservist, 4 on Veteran): move, evacuate, fortify, air-drop, raise militia, air strike, or cut a road or bridge.
- **Gauges:** public order and government confidence. Confidence of 80 or more gives an extra order; below 30 costs one; 0 ends the game.
- **Score:** share of the population saved. Shipped out counts fully, people waiting on a quay count 75%, people at home behind a held line count 50%.
- **Grades:** 58%, 46% and 32% thresholds.
- **Scenarios:** Kessler (designed) or a random outbreak; an optional seed replays a campaign.
- **Saving:** browser local storage (`SAVE_KEY` for the campaign, `PREF_KEY` for preferences).

Keys: `E` end turn, `U` undo, `Esc` cancel, `?` field manual, `+`/`−` zoom.
