# How to play the City

**Play:** https://turbulenceking5.github.io/Last-boat-out/city/

You command the defence of one city in the first night of an outbreak. The infected break out of St Brennan's Hospital on the east side. You hold Fort Aldermoor in the north-west corner and the harbour along the south shore. **The last ship sails after 12 minutes.** Get as many people out as you can.

## The loop

1. **Open evacuation routes.** The harbour is open from the start. Buy bus evacuation points to add capacity closer to where people live.
2. **Move people.** Order blocks to evacuate, or let them panic when the infected come close. Use APCs to carry people out of blocks that are cut off.
3. **Fight.** Buy troops and vehicles, put them where the infected are coming from, and call in air support when a horde gets too big.
4. **React.** Missions appear on the map, new outbreaks start, and at minute 8 a great horde arrives from the east.

## Winning

You are scored on the share of the city's people who got out (see [Money and scoring](07-money-and-scoring.md)):

| Saved | Grade |
|---|---|
| 60% or more | Saviour of the city |
| 45% or more | An orderly withdrawal |
| 30% or more | A bitter retreat |
| Less | Catastrophe |

The game ends when the last ship sails, or earlier if nobody is left in the city.

## One figure, ten people

Every person on the map, whether civilian, infected or soldier, stands for 10 people. Numbers in the top bar and panels are in people, not figures.
