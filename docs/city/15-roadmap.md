# Roadmap

Ideas for the City, roughly in order of how much they would add.

## Play

- **Line of sight.** Troops shoot over buildings today. Blocking fire would make street corners matter.
- **Garrisons.** Put a squad inside a block to defend it, so it holds instead of emptying.
- **Box selection** and control groups (Ctrl+1 to 9).
- **Barricades and blown bridges**, as in the original: spend money to block a street.
- **Night.** Darkness after a set time, with searchlights and flares, as in the original game.
- **More missions:** escort a bus convoy. (Holding a block and rescuing a VIP are in.)
- **Scenarios and difficulty:** different outbreak sites, cities and harbour capacities.

## Feel

- **Music** that rises with the infected count.
- **More decisions**, and decisions that depend on how the night is going. (Four fixed ones are in, as are unit barks.)
- **A minimap.**

## Sharing and keeping

- Save and resume a game in progress.
- A service record of past games and seeds, like the original's.
- Share links that open a seed.

## Technical

- Run the simulation in a Web Worker for big hordes on slow phones.
- Balance against real play: the numbers so far come from scripted players only.
