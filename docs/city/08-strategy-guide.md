# Strategy guide

What works, according to the scripted players in `tests/city.js` (4 seeded games each):

| Approach | Saved |
|---|---|
| Do nothing | 19.7% |
| Evacuate every block at once | 32.1% |
| Steady: bus points early, evacuate in waves, infantry screening the east, a tank and gunships | 51.7% |
| Active: steady, plus chasing every mission and running two APCs | 64.5% |

## Lessons

1. **The harbour alone caps you at about 32%.** It can ship only 700 people every 40 seconds. Bus points are what make a good score possible: buy several early, spread across the city.
2. **Don't empty the city all at once.** Evacuating everything floods the streets and the evacuation points, and crowds draw the infected. Evacuate in waves, starting with blocks nearest the danger.
3. **Hold the bridges.** The outbreak starts at St Brennan's, across the River Calder, and the great horde comes from the east. Everything east of the river reaches the city over four bridges; infantry at the west end of a bridge face the infected in single file while people get out behind them.
4. **Chase missions.** Supply drops pay for a tank; survivors are people you wouldn't otherwise save. Together with APC rescues, they were worth about 13 points over steady play.
5. **Use APCs on stuck blocks.** A block whose doors are besieged can't evacuate on its own, and an APC parked at a free door can still get 60 people out at a time.
6. **Save money for minute 8.** A gunship or an air strike on the great horde while it's still in the empty eastern streets kills hundreds without hurting anyone.
7. **Guard the crowds.** A bus point with 300 people waiting and no troops is a feeding ground.
8. **Air strikes kill civilians.** Bomb hordes in empty streets, not near queues or populated blocks.
