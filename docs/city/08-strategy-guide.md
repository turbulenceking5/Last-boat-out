# Strategy guide

What works, according to the scripted players in `tests/city.js` (4 seeded games each; 10 games in brackets):

| Approach | Saved |
|---|---|
| Do nothing | 18.7% (17.9%) |
| Evacuate every block at once | 32.1% (32.1%) |
| Steady: bus points early, evacuate in waves, infantry screening the east, a tank and gunships | 52.7% (49.9%) |
| Missions: steady, plus chasing every mission and running two APCs | 58.1% (59.4%) |
| Active: missions, plus answering decisions, using abilities, taking VIPs home and reinforcing | 61.7% (61.5%) |

Single games vary a lot, from about 40% to 75% for the same player, depending on where the outbreaks start.

## Lessons

1. **The harbour alone caps you at about 32%.** It can ship only 700 people every 40 seconds. Bus points are what make a good score possible: buy several early, spread across the city.
2. **Don't empty the city all at once.** Evacuating everything floods the streets and the evacuation points, and crowds draw the infected. Evacuate in waves, starting with blocks nearest the danger. The end screen says where people died: if most died in the streets, slow down.
3. **Screen the east.** The outbreak starts at St Brennan's in the east and the great horde comes from the east. Infantry on the streets west of the hospital slow the spread while people get out behind them.
4. **Chase missions.** Supply drops pay for a tank; survivors are people you wouldn't otherwise save; a VIP adds 400 places on the ships; a held block arms 60 of its residents. In the last six minutes they come thick and fast: keep a couple of fast units free for them.
5. **Press F whenever a fight starts.** Grenades and barrages never hurt civilians, so there's no reason to hold them back. Using every ability was worth about 4 points to the scripted player.
6. **Use the APC siren as bait.** Park an APC in front of your infantry or a tank and switch the siren on: everything within 26 cells walks into the guns. Or drive it away from a crowd that's being eaten, and the infected follow.
7. **Keep your veterans alive.** An elite squad shoots 16 points straighter and 30% faster. Pull a battered squad back to an evacuation point and reinforce it, rather than buying a new one.
8. **Use APCs on stuck blocks.** A block whose doors are besieged can't evacuate on its own, and an APC parked at a free door can still get 60 people out at a time.
9. **Decisions.** Telling Radio Calder to send people to the evacuation points helps if you have bus points open; paying for the Marguerite is worth it if the harbour queue is long late in the game; blowing the bridges is a bargain if the east is already empty.
10. **Save money for minute 8.** The great horde makes for the biggest crowd. A gunship or an air strike on it while it's still in the empty eastern streets kills hundreds without hurting anyone.
11. **Guard the crowds.** A bus point with 300 people waiting and no troops is a feeding ground.
12. **Air strikes kill civilians.** Bomb hordes in empty streets, not near queues or populated blocks.
13. **Fall back at the end.** Troops on the quay when the last ship sails come home, and so does anyone aboard an APC there.
