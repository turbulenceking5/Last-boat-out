# The map

The city is 74 by 70 cells. Roads 2 cells wide run every 8 cells; between them are 9 by 7 blocks of 6 by 6 cells. The quay runs along the south (rows 58 to 63) with the sea beyond. Streets, parks, the quay and Fort Aldermoor are walkable; buildings are not.

The street layout is the same every game. Block names, the outbreak timing and the missions vary with the seed (add `#seed=123` to the address to replay one).

## Districts

| District | Where | People per block (±20%) | Buildings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downtown | The middle (columns 4 to 6, rows 3 to 5, except Victory Park) | about 1,400 | One or two towers, 6 to 16 storeys |
| Residential | Most of the west and centre | about 900 | Four apartment blocks |
| Suburbs | The northern row and the western column | about 380 | Houses with gardens and trees |
| Industrial | The two eastern columns | about 140 | A shed and fuel tanks |
| Docks | The southern row | about 220 | Warehouses |
| St Brennan's Hospital | East side | about 650 | A large white hospital: where it starts |
| Parks | Victory Park (centre) and Millbrook Park (west) | none | Trees and paths; walkable |
| Fort Aldermoor | North-west corner | none | Barracks, helipad, sandbags; where units arrive |

The whole city holds about 38,800 people.

## Landmarks

- **The harbour:** the main evacuation point, in the middle of the quay; a freighter docks and sails.
- **Fort Aldermoor:** your base. Bought infantry, APCs and tanks drive out from here.
