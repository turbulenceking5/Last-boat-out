# Civilians and evacuation

## Sheltering and leaving

People stay in their block until either:

- you select the block and press **Evacuate**, or
- the infected come within 6 cells of one of its doors, and they panic.

An evacuating block lets 10 people out every 2 seconds, by the door nearest an evacuation point that has no infected outside it. If every door is blocked, or no evacuation point can be reached, they stay inside.

On the streets, civilians walk to the nearest evacuation point **with room**, stepping away from infected that come within about 2 cells.

## Evacuation points

| Point | Takes | Every | Holds | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The harbour | 700 | 40 s | 4,000 | Open from the start. The final ship takes one more load. |
| Bus point | 70 | 10 s | 350 | $300; on a street or the quay, at least 6 cells from another |

When a point is full, newcomers are sent to the next nearest one. When the last ship sails, every point takes one more load; anyone still waiting after that is left behind.

Crowds draw the infected. Once the infected reach one, they catch 10 people every 0.8 seconds.

## APC rescues

An APC can carry 60 people.

1. Select the APC and tap a block with people in it. It drives to the nearest door.
2. Parked there, it takes 10 people aboard every half second until it's full or the block is empty.
3. Tap near an evacuation point. When it arrives, everyone aboard joins the queue.

This gets people out of blocks whose doors are besieged or that have no safe route. If the APC is destroyed, everyone aboard is lost.

## Militia

Raising militia ($100) takes 60 people from a block and arms them. Survivors leave with the last ship and count as saved.
