# Missions, decisions and the great horde

## Orders from HQ

For the first four minutes the Missions box also lists four first orders, a short tutorial with small rewards. Each pays when you do it; when all four are done, HQ leaves you to it.

| Order | Reward |
|---|---|
| Open a bus evacuation point | $150 |
| Order a block to evacuate | $100 |
| Send troops (not militia) within 15 cells of St Brennan's Hospital | $150 |
| Use a unit's special ability | $100 |

## Missions

From about 0:55, and then every 55 to 80 seconds while fewer than two are active, a mission appears away from Fort Aldermoor and the evacuation points. Once 6:00 is left on the clock they come every 26 to 40 seconds, up to three at a time: people who hid are coming out onto the rooftops. None start in the last 75 seconds. Each shows as a beam of light with a pulsing ring, in the **Missions** list with a countdown, and as a message in the dispatches. **Show** moves the camera there.

| Mission | Chance (early / late) | Time | Reward | To complete |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hold a block | 15% / none | 90 s | $400 and a free militia company | Keep any unit within 6.5 cells of the block's centre for 30 seconds in all |
| VIP | 15% / 30% | 75 s to reach | $800, and 400 extra places on the coming sailings | Reach them with any unit, then bring that unit to the harbour or Fort Aldermoor |
| Survivors | 38% / 50% | 75 s | $400 and 60 to 90 people (80 to 140 late) | Get any unit to them before the infected do |
| Supply drop | 32% / 20% | 60 s | $700 | Get any unit within about 1.6 cells of the crate |

**Hold** picks a block with at least 300 people and infected within 16 cells. A ring round the block turns green while a unit is there; the panel shows the seconds held. When it's done the doors are barricaded (they hold twice as long) and 60 residents form a militia company. If the block falls first, the mission fails.

**VIPs** are a doctor, the harbourmaster, a councillor or a professor. Once picked up, they ride with the unit, the mission's beacon moves to the harbour, and the unit panel says "VIP aboard". If the unit is wiped out, the VIP is lost. Until they're reached, they draw the infected like any survivor.

**Survivors**:

- are added to the city's population when they appear, so losing them counts against your score
- draw the infected like any crowd, and are caught 10 at a time once the infected reach them
- climb into an APC if one reaches them and has room; otherwise they walk to the nearest evacuation point

If time runs out, the supply drop is lost and any survivors still waiting are counted as dead.

## Decisions

Four times a night, command asks you to decide something. The game pauses and a card shows the options; click one or press its number. Options you can't afford are greyed out with the reason.

| Time left | Decision | Options |
|---|---|---|
| 10:50 | **Radio Calder**: what to tell people | *Stay indoors*: for 2 minutes, blocks only leave when you order them to. *Make for the evacuation points*: every block within 20 cells of an evacuation point starts leaving now |
| 8:40 | **The dockers' union**: 200 dock workers want to help | *Arm them*: two free militia companies on the quay. *Put them on the ships*: 120 people join the harbour queue. Either way, the 120 are added to the population |
| 7:10 | **The Marguerite**: a ferry can make one extra run | *Pay $1,500*: 600 extra places on the coming sailings. *Commandeer her*: free, but the infantry squad nearest the harbour goes aboard and leaves the city. *Let her go* |
| 5:20 | **The river bridges**: the great horde is on the far bank | *Blow the bridges*: all four bridges over the River Calder go into the water, so nothing crosses again. The horde arrives at half strength, but nobody still east of the river can get out. *Mine the roads* ($800): the horde loses a third. *Keep them open* |

If nobody answers within 30 seconds of game time (only possible in the headless tests, since the page pauses), nothing happens.

## The great horde

| Time left | What happens |
|---|---|
| 4:30 | A siren sounds, a banner warns that the horde is 30 seconds out, and the dispatches report it crossing the river |
| 4:00 | 220 infected (2,200 people's worth; fewer if you blew the bridges or mined the roads) appear along the eastern edge: about 24% runners and 6% brutes |

The horde marches on the biggest crowd waiting at an evacuation point, if one has 300 or more people; otherwise on the centre of the city. The banner and the dispatches say which. Each infected breaks off when it comes within hunting range of someone. Meet it with tanks, a gunship, an air strike or an APC's siren while it is still in the empty eastern streets.

## The last ship

| Time left | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1:30 | A horn and a banner: fall back to the harbour |
| 0:30 | A second horn |
| 0:10 to 0:01 | A countdown in the banner, with a tick each second |

When she sails, troops within 12 cells of the harbour go with her (the end screen counts them as "Troops home"), and so does anyone aboard an APC there.
