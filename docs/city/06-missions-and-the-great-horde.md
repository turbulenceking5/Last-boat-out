# Missions and the great horde

## Missions

From about 0:55, and then every 55 to 80 seconds while fewer than two are active, a mission appears on a street away from Fort Aldermoor and the evacuation points. None start in the last 75 seconds. Each shows as a beam of light with a pulsing ring, in the **Missions** list with a countdown, and as a message in the dispatches. **Show** moves the camera to it.

| Mission | Time | Reward | To complete |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supply drop | 60 s | $700 | Get any unit within about 1.6 cells of the crate |
| Survivors | 75 s | $400 and 60 to 90 people | Get any unit to them before the infected do |

Survivors:

- are added to the city's population when they appear, so losing them counts against your score
- draw the infected like any crowd, and are caught 10 at a time once the infected reach them
- climb into an APC if one reaches them and has room; otherwise they walk to the nearest evacuation point

If time runs out, the supply drop is lost and any survivors still waiting are counted as dead.

## The great horde

| Time left | What happens |
|---|---|
| 4:30 | A siren sounds, a banner warns that the horde is 30 seconds out, and the dispatches report it crossing the river |
| 4:00 | 220 infected (2,200 people's worth) appear along the eastern edge: about 24% runners and 6% brutes |

The horde marches toward the centre of the city. Each infected breaks off when it comes within hunting range of someone. Meet it with tanks, a gunship, or an air strike while it is still in the empty eastern streets.
