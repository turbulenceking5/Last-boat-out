# The infected

## Kinds

| Kind | Looks | Health | Speed | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Walker | Shambling, grey-green head | 3 | 0.55 to 1.0 | Hunts people within 18 cells |
| Runner | Red shirt, leaning into a sprint | 1.5 | 1.6 to 2.0 | Notices people from 26 cells, goes down fast |
| Brute | 1.6 times the size, dark | 16 | 0.45 to 0.6 | Batters doors 6 times as fast; hits soldiers for 2 and vehicles for 5 |

Runners and brutes get commoner as the night goes on: brutes rise from 2% to 5% of new infected, runners from 6% to 16%. All infected get faster over the 12 minutes, up to 35% by the end.

## What they go for

The infected head for the nearest of:

- anyone in the streets
- your troops and vehicles
- crowds waiting at evacuation points
- survivors waiting for rescue
- the doors of blocks with people inside, but only from 8 cells away, so a quiet street doesn't call the whole horde

Beyond that range they wander.

## How they spread

- **Bites.** An infected next to a civilian bites. The victim lies on the ground for 10 to 18 seconds, then rises as a new infected.
- **Blocks.** At a populated block's door, the infected batter it for 25 seconds of combined effort (brutes count 6 times). Once it gives, the people inside are caught 10 at a time, every 5 seconds. A block whose people are all caught is **overrun**: it turns dark and smokes.
- **Evacuation points.** The infected at a crowd catch 10 people every 0.8 seconds.
- **New outbreaks** start at 2:30, 5:20 and 8:20 in a random populated block, with 8 infected each.

## The great horde

At 7:30 a siren sounds and a banner warns that a great horde is 30 seconds out. At 8:00, 220 infected, with extra runners and brutes, appear on the eastern roads and march toward the centre of the city until they find someone. See [Missions and the great horde](06-missions-and-the-great-horde.md).

## Limits

At most 2,400 infected figures (24,000 people) exist at once. Past that, people caught still die, but no new figures appear.
