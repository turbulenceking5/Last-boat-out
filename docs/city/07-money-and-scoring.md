# Money and scoring

## Income

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| City budget | $10 a second (game time) |
| Each infected killed | $3 |
| Each person evacuated by bus or ship | $0.60 |
| Supply drop | $700 |
| Survivors reached | $400 |
| Block held | $400, plus a free militia company |
| VIP brought to the harbour or the fort | $800, plus 400 extra places on the coming sailings |
| Orders from HQ (first four minutes) | $100 to $150 each, $500 in all |

You start with $1,500 and two infantry squads.

| Evac point | $300 |
| Air strike | $500 |
| APC | $700 |
| Gunship | $1,000 |
| Tank | $1,200 |
| Reinforcing or repairing | 60% of the price of what was lost |
| Paying the Marguerite (a decision) | $1,500 |
| Mining the eastern roads (a decision) | $800 |

The build bar shows a price in red when you can't afford it, and the game won't enter placement mode for it.

## Score

**Share saved = people evacuated ÷ the city's population.** The population is about 38,800 people, plus any survivors found by missions and the 120 dockers of the dockers' union decision.

Counted as evacuated:

- everyone who left on a bus or ship
- one final load from every evacuation point when the last ship sails
- militia still alive at the end
- anyone aboard an APC within 12 cells of the harbour when the last ship sails

Not counted: anyone still in a block, on the streets, or waiting at an evacuation point beyond that last load.

| Saved | Grade |
|---|---|
| 60% or more | Saviour of the city |
| 45% or more | An orderly withdrawal |
| 30% or more | A bitter retreat |
| Less | Catastrophe |

The end screen also shows the dead, infected destroyed, units lost, money spent, missions completed, people rescued by APC and troops brought home on the last ship; where people died (in the streets, waiting at evacuation points, in their homes, under your own air strikes, elsewhere); and the unit of the night, the one with the most kills.
