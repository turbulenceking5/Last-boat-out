# Money and scoring

## Income

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| City budget | $10 a second (game time) |
| Each infected killed | $3 |
| Each person evacuated by bus or ship | $0.60 |
| Supply drop | $700 |
| Survivors reached | $400 |

You start with $1,500 and two infantry squads.

## Costs

| Order | Cost |
|---|---|
| Militia | $100 |
| Infantry squad | $300 |
| Evac point | $300 |
| Air strike | $500 |
| APC | $700 |
| Gunship | $1,000 |
| Tank | $1,200 |

The build bar shows a price in red when you can't afford it, and the game won't enter placement mode for it.

## Score

**Share saved = people evacuated ÷ the city's population.** The population is about 37,500 people, plus any survivors found by missions.

Counted as evacuated:

- everyone who left on a bus or ship
- one final load from every evacuation point when the last ship sails
- militia still alive at the end

Not counted: anyone still in a block, on the streets, or waiting at an evacuation point beyond that last load.

| Saved | Grade |
|---|---|
| 60% or more | Saviour of the city |
| 45% or more | An orderly withdrawal |
| 30% or more | A bitter retreat |
| Less | Catastrophe |

The end screen also shows the dead, infected destroyed, units lost, money spent, missions completed and people rescued by APC.
